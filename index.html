Best AI tools for ecommerce

Buy a job, not a category. ChatGPT already writes a passable PDP. The tools below earn a seat only when they publish back, batch, or sit on store data you cannot paste into a chat window.

Verdict

Start with what you already pay for: Shopify Magic and Sidekick if you are on Shopify, plus ChatGPT or Claude for one-off copy. Add a specialist only when a job breaks: bulk catalog publish-back, listing images, ticketed support, lifecycle email/SMS, or Amazon research. Do not buy a marketing writer to run a 2,000-SKU catalog. Do not buy a GEO suite to write product titles.

One primary tool per job. That is the whole method.

Do not buy if

  • You want one AI subscription to “run the store.” That product does not exist.
  • Your catalog is under 50 SKUs and ChatGPT already drafts PDPs you will edit anyway.
  • You have not used Shopify Magic, Sidekick, and Inbox for a week.
  • You are buying Writesonic or Jasper to bulk-load a PIM. Neither is a catalog system.
  • You need a helpdesk with in-ticket refunds and you are looking at a chat widget.

Start here by store size

  • Pre-revenue / < $10k GMV a month: native Shopify AI + ChatGPT + phone photos through Photoroom. Zero extra seats.
  • $10–50k: add a paid Photoroom plan if listings are the bottleneck; add Omnisend when email is more than a blast; add Tidio when Inbox misses tickets.
  • $50k+ or 200+ SKUs: a dedicated catalog tool (see the description-generator page). Decide helpdesk vs chat. Stop using a marketing writer as a PIM.
  • Amazon-led: Helium 10 on the plan that matches their published sales bands, plus Photoroom. Listing copy is a separate, Amazon-safe pass.

Catalog and product copy

Two different products hide under “AI writer.” A marketing writer (Jasper, Writesonic, Copy.ai, Anyword, ChatGPT) is built for campaigns, brand voice, and pages you touch one at a time. A catalog system (Describely, Hypotenuse, and Shopify Magic at small scale) is built for attributes, bulk import, and publish-back. Mixing them up is how stores buy Jasper and still have 1,400 empty SKUs.

Under 50 SKUs: stay on Shopify Magic or a ChatGPT project with a pasted style sheet. Magic is included with Shopify. It is one-at-a-time. That is fine at this size.

50–500 SKUs: the cost question is “what is the cost per live PDP,” not “what is the seat.” Describely publishes pay-as-you-go at $0.75 per product (first five free) as of 21 August 2026 — that math is honest at a few hundred SKUs. Hypotenuse lists Basic for catalogs under 100 products and Enterprise for large teams; both are custom priced. Check current pricing.

500+ SKUs: you need publish-back, attribute enrichment, and a review queue. That is Hypotenuse’s Enterprise story, Describely’s high-volume custom tier, or a real PIM with a writer bolted on. It is not Jasper Pro.

Jasper Pro, read on jasper.ai/pricing on 21 August 2026: $69 per seat / month, or $59 per seat / month billed yearly. Business is custom. Writesonic’s public ladder the same day: Starter $79/mo billed annually, Basic $199, Growth $399, Enterprise custom — and the product is now an AI-search visibility platform with article limits, not a catalog PIM. Full matrix: best AI product description generators. If you are on Jasper and shopping: Jasper alternatives.

Product photography and listing images

Split the job before you buy: packshot cleanup (background, dust, crop) is not lifestyle scene generation, which is not on-model fashion. Photoroom covers the first two for most Shopify and marketplace sellers. Their public pricing page on 21 August 2026 lists Pro / Max / Ultra / Enterprise on a credit-and-export model (example: Pro 8,000 AI credits and 1,000 exports on yearly billing). The dollar figures did not survive a static read of that page. Check current Photoroom pricing.

Claid is a catalog/API-shaped alternative. We did not capture a first-party affiliate page for Claid, so it is text only. A real camera still wins glass, jewelry, fabric hand, and anything a marketplace will reject as a misleading composite. Read Amazon and Google image rules before you generate a lifestyle scene you cannot defend.

Paid ads creative

Do not buy generation, scoring, and spy/research on day one. Most stores should ship: a clean PDP image from Photoroom or a phone, copy from ChatGPT or the writer they already have, and Meta’s own Advantage+ / catalog ads. Canva is the free-enough layout layer.

AdCreative.ai sits in this category. Semrush lists it as a commissionable product on their official affiliate page — we will not promote it through that program until the site clears Semrush’s published quality bar. If you evaluate it, use adcreative.ai directly. Jasper and Writesonic can draft primary text; they do not replace a designer or a media buyer.

On-site support and chat

Do not mix these three: a chat widget, a helpdesk, and Shopify Inbox. Inbox is included in the Shopify universe and is the right first install. Suggested replies in Inbox are a composing aid for staff, not an autopilot refund bot — see Shopify’s Inbox Magic notes.

Tidio is chat-first with Lyro as the AI agent. Official pricing on 21 August 2026 is usage-metered: Starter at $24.17/mo on annual billing (100 billable conversations), Growth starting at $49.17/mo, Plus from $300/mo, Lyro as an add-on from $32.50/mo (from 50 AI conversations). A “billable conversation” is one a human agent replies to — read their FAQ on that page before you model cost. Check current Tidio pricing.

Gorgias is a helpdesk: tickets, Shopify order actions, a different AI-agent bill. We have not captured an official Gorgias affiliate URL, so there is no Gorgias CTA here. If your agents issue refunds and edit orders in-ticket, you are shopping a helpdesk, not a chat app. Compare them on a later page; until then, pick by daily tickets, not by “AI chatbot” blog titles.

Email and SMS

Platform first. AI second. A subject-line generator on top of a weak lifecycle setup is theater.

Omnisend is ecommerce email + SMS + push, with AI for forms, send time, and product blocks. Official pricing on 21 August 2026: a Free plan at $0 (250 contacts, 500 emails/month), then Standard and Pro that scale with list size. The page is a calculator — we saw a Standard example at the 500-contact tier and a Pro example at 2,500 contacts, including a 3-month promo. SMS starting rate on that page: $0.007 at high US volume. Check current Omnisend pricing for your list.

Klaviyo is the other default for Shopify stores. It is not an open blog-affiliate program — K:Partners is an application. We will not invent a Klaviyo referral link. Kit (kit.com) is the creator-email alternative if the “store” is actually a newsletter with merch.

Amazon / marketplace selling

Four jobs: research, listing, ads, ops. Helium 10’s public pricing page on 21 August 2026 no longer sells a cheap Starter — the live ladder is Platinum $129/mo or $99/mo yearly (their band: $0–$100k yearly sales), Diamond $359/mo or $279/mo yearly ($100k–$10M), and Enterprise from $1,499/mo billed annually. Diamond is where they put AI listing builder and the MCP connector. Check current Helium 10 pricing.

Jungle Scout is the usual comparison. We did not capture an official Jungle Scout affiliate URL, so no CTA. Photoroom still does the image job. Listing copy must stay Amazon-safe: no “best,” no “#1,” no medical claims you cannot back. ChatGPT will happily write those words. That is a you problem, not a model problem.

What ChatGPT already replaced (and what it did not)

Replaced, for most stores: first-draft PDP paragraphs, collection blurbs, ad primary text variants, FAQ answers you will still fact-check, and “rewrite this email in our voice” once you paste three examples.

Did not replace: bulk publish-back to Shopify or a PIM; brand governance across a team (this is Jasper’s actual remaining pitch); AI-search visibility tracking (Writesonic’s current pitch); conversation-metered support with Shopify order actions; lifecycle email/SMS on store events; Amazon keyword research on live marketplace data; marketplace-legal image sizes at batch scale.

If your only pain is “I need a paragraph,” you do not have a software problem. You have a paste-into-ChatGPT problem. The $20 ChatGPT or Claude plan is the correct “writer” until a job above shows up.
