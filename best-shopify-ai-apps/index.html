Best Shopify AI apps

Most “15 best Shopify AI apps” posts are written by an app in the list. Start with what you already pay for. Then add at most three paid apps, each tied to a job.

Updated 21 August 2026 · Official Shopify and vendor pages · No invented App Store ratings

Affiliate disclosure. Some links may become affiliate links when programs are approved. Official URLs only. Shopify Affiliate is the platform CTA. Omnisend, Tidio, and Photoroom have public programs; we have not attached IDs. We do not invent programs for every app we mention.

Verdict

Install nothing until you have used Shopify Magic, Sidekick, and Inbox for a week. Then add, at most: Photoroom if listing images are the bottleneck, Omnisend if email/SMS is the bottleneck, Tidio if chat volume outgrows Inbox. A catalog writer is a different decision — see product description generators. Skip ChatGPT-wrapper apps that resell a prompt inside the App Store.

Do not buy if

  • The app’s homepage ranks itself #1 in its own roundup. Read the byline.
  • You have not opened Sidekick. It is included with your Shopify plan; features and usage limits vary by plan.
  • You want the bot to issue store credit with no rule. That is how you lose money.
  • You are stacking a writer, a page builder, an upsell app, and a chatbot in the same afternoon.

Start here by store size

  • New store: native only. Magic for PDPs and email drafts, Sidekick for admin, Inbox for chat.
  • Live catalog, ugly photos: native + Photoroom.
  • Repeat customers, weak flows: native + Omnisend (or stay on Klaviyo if you already live there — no fake Klaviyo link).
  • Daily tickets: Inbox until it breaks, then Tidio (chat-first) or a helpdesk, not both.

Native first — Magic, Sidekick, Inbox

You already paid for these. They are the baseline every paid app has to beat.

Shopify Magic

Official help: Magic is a free suite of AI features across Shopify products. Shopify says generally available features are on all plans and in all languages; some surfaces (especially early access) vary. It drafts in context — product copy, email, and Inbox suggested replies among other admin jobs. It is not a bulk catalog system. Limits that matter for operators: one-at-a-time writing, and brand controls that live in your prompt and your store content, not in a separate voice graph.

Sidekick

Official product page: Sidekick is an AI commerce assistant with access to store context and admin actions. Shopify’s own FAQ: included with your Shopify plan; features and usage limits vary by plan. It respects staff permissions. Spring ’26 Edition notes describe Sidekick working with selected partner apps, multi-tasking, and an AI sales associate via Inbox. Use it for admin questions and small actions. It does not replace a helpdesk, a lifecycle email platform, an ads stack, or a 2,000-SKU writer. When those jobs appear, buy those products — do not wait for Sidekick to grow a new organ.

Inbox

Shopify Inbox is the native messaging tool. Magic’s suggested replies are a composing aid for staff, not automatic refunds. Shopify’s help page (English-only for auto-suggested replies) requires the store and admin in English, Inbox installed with at least one conversation, and 200,000 products or fewer. If you need an AI agent that talks without a human, you have left native and you are shopping Tidio / a helpdesk AI — with hallucination and refund risk on you.

How we scored

Four filters, no stars we did not read from the App Store this week:

  1. Job. One sentence: what breaks if we uninstall it.
  2. Native overlap. If Magic / Sidekick / Inbox already do 80%, the app has to justify the remainder.
  3. Starting price. Official public page only, dated. If the App Store tile disagrees with the vendor site, believe neither until you open a cart — and we will not invent the number.
  4. Data access. Read/write on products, orders, customers. A wrapper that only sees the page you are on is a prompt, not an app.

We did not paste Shopify App Store ratings into this page. Ratings move. Vendor listicles cherry-pick them. Open the live listing the week you install.

Catalog and SEO apps

For copy, prefer Magic until 50 SKUs, then a dedicated catalog tool — not a “SEO AI” app that rewrites titles in a loop. Describely and Hypotenuse are covered on the description-generator page. They may or may not be App Store apps on the week you read this; buy them for publish-back, not for a badge.

Shopify SEO apps (compressors, schema helpers, collection SEO) are a separate job from “AI writer.” We are not dumping SEOWILL / TinyIMG / Webrex into a ranked list without a dated App Store read. If you need one, search the official Shopify App Store and check the last update date.

Photos and page builders

Photoroom is the image layer we will actually name: background removal, packshots, AI backgrounds, fashion models, Shopify integration listed on their official pricing grid. Plans on 21 August 2026: Pro / Max / Ultra / Enterprise on credits and exports. Dollar amounts did not come through a static read. Check current Photoroom pricing. That is a better first paid photo app than a page-builder that also “does AI images.”

Page builders with an AI button are usually Magic-or-ChatGPT in a sidebar. If you already have a theme you can edit, skip them. If you do not, pick a builder for the builder, not for the button.

AOV / recommendations

Recommendation and bundle apps love to title themselves “best AI app.” Native merchandising, Shopify search, and a well-built collection often cover a young store. We will not rank Rebuy, Fast Bundle, or similar here: several of the 2026 listicles ranking “best Shopify AI apps” are written by those vendors. Independence means we wait for a hands-on pass, not that we recycle their table.

Support

Inbox first. Then choose a shape:

  • Chat-first, SMB volume: Tidio (Lyro AI agent + live chat + light helpdesk). Official pricing 21 August 2026: Free tier exists; Starter $24.17/mo annual (100 billable conversations); Growth from $49.17/mo; Plus from $300/mo; Lyro add-on from $32.50/mo. Usage is metered. Check current Tidio pricing. App Store listing: apps.shopify.com/tidio-chat.
  • Helpdesk, order actions, larger ticket volume: Gorgias is the usual name. No official affiliate URL in our research, so no CTA. Read gorgias.com if that is your job.

Never let an agent issue refunds or store credit unless you have written the rule and tested a malicious prompt. “AI chatbot for Shopify” posts skip this because it does not convert.

Email / SMS

Omnisend is ecommerce email + SMS + push, with Shopify as a first-class connection. Official pricing 21 August 2026: Free at $0 (250 contacts, 500 emails/mo); Standard and Pro scale with list size (calculator on the page — we will not freeze one promo cell as “the” price). SMS starting at $0.007 on high-volume US sends per that page. AI features they publish: forms, send time, product recommendations, subject/assistant copy. Check current Omnisend pricing. App Store: apps.shopify.com/omnisend.

Klaviyo remains the other default. It is a partner program, not an open affiliate link. If you already run Klaviyo, switching for “AI subject lines” is a bad reason. Switching for bill shape or SMS packaging can be a real reason — compare invoices on the two official pricing pages the week you do it.

Omnisend — official site

Ads

Meta and Google already sit on your catalog. Shopify’s own campaigns and native pixels do more than a third “AI ads” app for most stores under $50k GMV. Photoroom supplies creative. ChatGPT or your writer supplies primary text. We are not adding AdCreative.ai as a Shopify app recommendation on day one; if you evaluate it, use adcreative.ai and do not expect a Semrush-network link from us until that program’s quality bar is real.

Apps we skipped (wrapper-on-ChatGPT)

If the App Store screenshots are a prompt box, a “tone” dropdown, and a credit meter, it is ChatGPT with a Shopify session. You already have ChatGPT. You already have Magic. Paying $9.99/mo plus usage to sit between them is how app stacks get to $400 without a job getting done.

Also skipped on this cut: any app whose primary public artifact is a “best Shopify AI apps 2026” post that ranks itself first (the Alia and Fast Bundle examples in current SERPs). We may review the products later. We will not launder the listicle.

A sane starter stack under three paid apps

Three paid apps is a ceiling, not a goal. Native does not count toward the three. August 2026.
SeatWhatWhy it earns a slot
0 (native) Magic + Sidekick + Inbox Copy drafts, admin, first-line chat. Already on the invoice.
Paid 1 Photoroom or a catalog writer — pick the actual bottleneck Images or SKU intake. Not both on week one.
Paid 2 Omnisend or Klaviyo (if you are already there) Lifecycle. Only when you will ship welcome / abandon / post-purchase.
Paid 3 Tidio or a helpdesk Only when Inbox drops chats or you need order actions in-ticket.

If you need a fourth paid AI app, the problem is usually process, not software. Re-read the job-based stack.
